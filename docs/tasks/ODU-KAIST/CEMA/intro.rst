Introduction to CEMA
====================

**Chemical Explosive Mode Analysis (CEMA)** is an advanced mathematical diagnostic tool used in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to analyze complex chemical kinetic behaviors in turbulent combustion simulations. It systematically identifies local combustion regimes, such as auto-ignition, flame propagation fronts, and local extinction/quenching.

Core Concept & Mathematical Formulation
---------------------------------------

CEMA operates on the principle that local chemical reactivity can be evaluated by analyzing the eigenvalues of the chemical Jacobian matrix.
