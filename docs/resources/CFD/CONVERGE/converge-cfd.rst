What is CONVERGE CFD?
=====================

CONVERGE CFD is a multi-purpose Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) software package developed by Convergent Science. It simulates 3D fluid flows, heat transfer, turbulence, and chemical reactions for complex engineering applications.

Key Features & Innovations
--------------------------
*   **Autonomous Meshing**: Unlike traditional CFD software that requires manual grid creation (which can take days or weeks), CONVERGE generates a Cartesian mesh automatically at runtime based on simple user-defined parameters.

*   **Adaptive Mesh Refinement (AMR)**: Dynamically refines or coarsens the computational grid during the simulation in response to local flow conditions—such as high velocity gradients, flame fronts, or temperature spikes.

*   **Cut-Cell Cartesian Method**: Accurately represents complex and moving geometries without deformation or loss of cell quality.

*   **Fully Coupled Chemistry (SAGE Solver)**: Solves detailed chemical kinetics directly within the fluid flow equations, making it industry-leading for combustion and reaction dynamics (e.g., e-fuels, hydrogen combustion, natural gas). 

*   **High-Performance Computing (HPC) Scaling**: Scales efficiently across hundreds to thousands of processor cores to minimize runtimes for complex transient simulations.
