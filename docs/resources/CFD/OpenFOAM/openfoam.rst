What is OpenFOAM?
=================

OpenFOAM (Open Field Operation and Manipulation) is a free, open-source C++ software suite for Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and general continuum mechanics. Originally developed at Imperial College London in the late 1980s and open-sourced under the GNU General Public License (GPLv3) in 2004, it serves as a powerful alternative to expensive commercial CFD packages like ANSYS Fluent or COMSOL
